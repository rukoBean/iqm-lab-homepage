## Who we are recruiting
{: .cta}

PhD students, master students, undergraduate research interns, and postdoctoral researchers.

## What we look for

- **Academic interest**: Interest in condensed matter physics, materials physics, computational materials, or quantum materials problems.
- **Willingness to learn**: Willing to learn first-principles calculations, model construction, numerical simulations, data analysis, and machine-learning methods.
- **Basic background**: A background in physics, materials, or computing; programming experience is a plus but not required.
{: .openings-requirements}

Curiosity, persistence, and willingness to learn are valued. Prior programming or simulation experience is helpful but not mandatory for junior students.
{: .muted}
