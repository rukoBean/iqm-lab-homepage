      <div class="hero-slogan">AI-enabled quantum materials discovery · Physics-guided rational materials design</div>
      <p class="lead">Our group works at the intersection of condensed matter physics, computational materials science, and artificial intelligence, developing multiscale theories and data-driven methods for complex quantum materials. We combine first-principles calculations, effective models, many-body numerical simulations, and machine learning to explore high-temperature superconductivity, strongly correlated electron systems, low-dimensional magnetism, multiferroicity, and magnetoelectric coupling. Our goal is to identify new mechanisms, predict novel properties, and discover new materials across vast materials spaces.</p>
      <p class="lead">We welcome students and collaborators with backgrounds in physics, materials science, computing, mathematics, or artificial intelligence to help build an open, interdisciplinary, and forward-looking research platform for intelligent quantum materials.</p>
